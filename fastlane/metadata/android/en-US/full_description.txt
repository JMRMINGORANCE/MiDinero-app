MiDinero logs your spending with almost no effort and helps you stay within budget every month. Everything is stored on your phone: no account, no Internet and no ads.

<b>Log without effort</b>
• Detects the payments you make with your phone (Google Wallet / Google Pay and, if you like, Samsung Wallet, Revolut, N26 and Spanish banks).
• Sorts them by merchant and learns when you correct it.
• Assign the category right from the payment notification, without opening the app.
• Add expenses and income by hand with autocomplete, or type “12.50 coffee yesterday” and you're done.
• Widget, Quick Settings tile and shortcuts.
• Import transactions from your bank's CSV without duplicates.
• Attach a photo of the receipt for warranties and returns.

<b>Recurring charges</b>
• Rent, Netflix, Spotify, your car payment or your salary: logged automatically on the due date.
• Weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly, with instalments that end by themselves and a reminder the day before.

<b>Budgets that warn you in time</b>
• A monthly budget and limits per category, with alerts before you go over.
• Tells you if you're spending too fast and how much you can spend per day.
• Suggested limits based on what you usually spend.
• Your month can start on payday.

<b>See where it goes</b>
• Comparison with last month, month-end forecast and 6-month trend.
• Breakdown by category, day of the week and top merchants.
• Income, savings and a weekly summary.

<b>Trips, friends and goals</b>
• Trips and events with their own budget: what you pay on those days is logged there automatically.
• Expenses in dollars, pounds and other currencies converted to euros.
• Share an expense with friends and keep track of who owes you.
• Savings goals with how much to put aside each month.

<b>On your Wear OS watch</b>
• What you can spend today on the watch face, a tile and a complication.
• Log expenses from your wrist with the keypad or by voice.

<b>Your data, yours</b>
• Automatic backup to the folder you choose, and export to Excel.
• Search any expense from any month, by text or amount.
• Lock with fingerprint or PIN, hide amounts and private screen.
• MiDinero has no Internet permission: it never sends your data to any server.
• In Spanish and English.
